    01The Club

    Undergraduate finance, practiced at professional standard.

    Rutgers Chinese Finance Club (RUCFC) is a finance collective at Rutgers University–New Brunswick, dedicated to the intersection of global capital markets, technology, and the Greater China economy.

    Founded in 2026 by a Finance + Computer Science student whose sell-side equity research covered the AI hardware supply chain, RUCFC is built on a single premise: undergraduate finance should be practiced at professional standard, not merely studied.

    Bridging Eastern and Western business cultures.

    02Programs

    Three disciplines, one standard.

    1. I

      Institutional-Grade Research

      Sector deep-dives and equity theses built on sell-side research methodology — from supply-chain channel checks to valuation.

    2. II

      Professional Credentialing

      Bloomberg Terminal, BMC, and ESG certification tracks, paired with AI-augmented analytical workflows.

    3. III

      Closed-Door Access

      Off-record sessions with frontline practitioners, and a close-knit, high-calibre peer network.

    03The Standard

    We hold ourselves to the standard of the profession we intend to enter.

    1. I

      Rigour

      Every view is supported by evidence, and every model can be defended line by line.

    2. II

      Conviction

      We form independent theses and are prepared to be measured against them.

    3. III

      Contribution

      Members are expected to give as much to the collective as they take from it.

    Founding members will shape the club's research agenda, standards, and culture from day one.
