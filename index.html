    01The Club

    Undergraduate finance, conducted to institutional standards.

    The Rutgers Chinese Finance Club (RCFC) is an undergraduate finance organization at Rutgers University–New Brunswick. Its work centres on global capital markets, technology-sector equities, and the macroeconomics of Greater China.

    RCFC was founded in 2026 by a Finance and Computer Science student with sell-side equity research experience covering the AI hardware supply chain. The club is organized around a single principle: undergraduate finance should be conducted to institutional standards, not studied in the abstract.

    A cross-border perspective on U.S. and Chinese capital markets.

    02Programs

    Three practice areas. One standard.

    1. I

      Equity Research

      Sector initiations and single-name investment theses built on sell-side methodology: industry mapping, supply-chain channel checks, financial modelling, and DCF and trading-comparables valuation.

    2. II

      Professional Credentialing

      Structured preparation for Bloomberg Market Concepts (BMC) and ESG certifications, alongside Bloomberg Terminal workflows and AI-assisted data analysis.

    3. III

      Closed-Door Sessions

      Off-the-record discussions with industry practitioners, supported by a peer network held to the same professional standard.

    03Our Standards

    We apply the professional standards of the industry we intend to enter.

    1. I

      Rigour

      Every view is evidence-based; every model is auditable line by line.

    2. II

      Conviction

      We develop independent investment theses and are accountable for their outcomes.

    3. III

      Contribution

      Each member contributes research and work product commensurate with what they draw from the club.

    Founding members will define the club's research agenda, analytical standards, and governance from inception.
